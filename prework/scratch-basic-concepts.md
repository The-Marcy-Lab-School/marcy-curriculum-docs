## Learn the Scratch concepts behind the requirements

**How to use this resource list**

Each concept below (if/else, loops, etc.) has a resource next to it. You don't need to read all of them — only open a resource when you're actually stuck on that concept.

1. **Hit a wall?** Find the concept that matches what you're trying to do, and check out its resource.
2. **Did it help?** Great — now find the *smallest possible piece* of your project you can get working using it. Don't try to build the whole feature at once. Get one tiny thing working, test it, then move to the next tiny thing.
3. **Still stuck?** Your question might be too big. Try shrinking it. Instead of "how do I make my character do this whole complicated thing," ask "how do I make one part of it happen." Solve that first.

The goal isn't to read every resource — it's to get unstuck just enough to keep building.

## Trusted video support

If you prefer video, start with the official Scratch Team channel:

- **Scratch Team YouTube channel:** https://www.youtube.com/scratchteam
    
    This is the official YouTube channel for Scratch. Look for beginner tutorials and short videos on specific features.
    

When watching a tutorial, do not just copy every block. Pause and ask:

1. What is this block doing?
2. What changed on the screen?
3. Where could I use this idea in my own project?
4. What would happen if I changed one value or condition?
